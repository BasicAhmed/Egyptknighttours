# Phase 1 – Research

Sources: live web search (Sept 2026) of tour marketplaces and Egypt operators. Patterns only; no branding or content is copied.

## What the marketplaces (GetYourGuide, Viator, Klook, Tiqets) do well
- **Discovery-to-booking is short.** Search, filter cards, tour page, date picker, pay. Cards show image, duration, rating, review count and "from" price.
- **Verified reviews at volume.** Reviews tied to a real booking are the main trust signal. Viator adds Tripadvisor reviews on many listings.
- **Free cancellation up to 24h and "reserve now, pay later"** (charged ~48–72h before the tour) remove booking risk. This is the single most copied pattern.
- **24/7 support** is advertised on every page.
- **Curated collections** (GYG "Originals") and rewards programs drive repeat use.
- **Mobile first.** Sticky price/CTA bar, date and traveler pickers in bottom sheets.

## The business opportunity for a direct operator
- OTAs take roughly **20% (Viator) to 30% (GetYourGuide)** commission at base rates, more if the operator pays to rank. OTAs' share of tour bookings grew from 24% (2019) to 33% (2024) per Arival's operator survey.
- Every booking won on our own site keeps that margin. So the site must beat the OTAs on trust and convenience while keeping the price honest. Sources: junglebee.com, automate.travel operator commission comparisons.

## Egypt competitors (what they do)
- **Emo Tours Egypt, Memphis Tours, Egypt Tours Portal, Rimo Tours, Real Egypt:** strong review counts (thousands on Tripadvisor / Google / Trustpilot), licensed by the Ministry of Tourism, Egyptologist guides, private vehicles, 24/7 support, tailor-made itineraries, Nile cruises, transfers.
- Most rely on heavy listicle SEO ("best Egypt tour companies") and dense schema (AggregateRating, Organization).
- Weaknesses seen: cluttered layouts, many near-duplicate tour pages, generic copy, WhatsApp as an afterthought, little real trip-planning tooling, no visible follow-up after an inquiry.

## Gaps Egypt Knight can exploit
1. **Clean, mobile-first booking flow** with a deposit option and honest all-in pricing.
2. **Trip builder + budget calculator** that captures leads instead of only a contact form.
3. **WhatsApp with context** (tour, dates, group size prefilled).
4. **Guide content that funnels to tours** (information → trust → booking), without thin pages.
5. **Real CRM follow-up** (day 0/1/3/5 cadence, pre-trip, post-trip review request).
6. **Distinct brand:** the Egypt Knight mascot and a warm, funny, human voice.

## Rules we hold ourselves to
- No fabricated reviews, ratings, awards, licence numbers or statistics. Launch with zero reviews and collect real ones; only add AggregateRating schema once real reviews exist.
- No fake timers or scarcity. Show "spots left" only from real availability data.
- Licence and registration details are placeholders until Egypt Knight supplies them.
